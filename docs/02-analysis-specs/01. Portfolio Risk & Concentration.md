# 01. Portfolio Risk & Concentration

## Status

Draft — awaiting review. First analysis module. Consumes derived positions from
`docs/01-data-sources/01. Ledger (Google Sheet).md` and prices from the price
source. Deterministic and auditable; no LLM, no price prediction.

## Why this module first

The owner's current real portfolio is ~100% semiconductor (SMH already holds MU
and SK Hynix, so the single-name positions double the exposure). This module
measures that concentration and the risk it implies, in numbers, so the weekly
report can state it with evidence rather than vibes.

## Inputs

- **Positions** (from the ledger): per-ticker `qty`, `avg_cost_usd`,
  `cost_basis_idr`, entry FX.
- **Current prices** (price source): latest close per ticker.
- **Current FX**: USD/IDR now.
- **Price history** (price source): ~1y daily closes per ticker (for volatility,
  drawdown, correlation).
- **Classification map** (static, in repo): ticker → sector / theme / asset
  class. See "Classification" below.
- **Benchmark**: a reference series (default `SMH` for the semi tilt and `VOO`
  for the broad market) for relative return.

## Outputs

A `PortfolioRisk` result containing:

1. **Allocation table** — per holding: market value (IDR), weight %, sector,
   theme, asset class.
2. **Concentration metrics:**
   - Top-1 and Top-3 holding weight %.
   - **Sector concentration** %: weight in the largest sector.
   - **Theme/factor exposure** %: weight exposed to a single factor
     (e.g. "memory-chip cycle"), including **look-through** of ETF holdings
     where known (SMH → semis; and flag the overlap that SMH holds SK Hynix).
   - **Herfindahl–Hirschman Index (HHI)** of weights (0–10000) as a single
     concentration number.
3. **Risk metrics (per holding and portfolio):**
   - Annualized **volatility** (stdev of daily returns × √252).
   - **Max drawdown** over the lookback window.
   - **Average pairwise correlation** among holdings (how much they move
     together — high = little real diversification).
4. **Relative return:** portfolio return vs each benchmark over 1w / 1m / YTD,
   so a drop is contextualized ("−4.4% while SMH −4.1% → in line with the
   sector, not idiosyncratic").
5. **Concentration flags** (rule-based, see Rules) with evidence + limitation
   text, in the evidence-first style.

## Classification

A static mapping committed to the repo (no PII), e.g.:

```
SMH   → sector: Information Technology, theme: Semiconductors (broad),  class: ETF
MU    → sector: Information Technology, theme: Memory/HBM,              class: Stock
SKHY  → sector: Information Technology, theme: Memory/HBM,              class: Stock
VOO   → sector: Broad market,           theme: Broad market,            class: ETF
GC=F  → sector: Commodity,              theme: Gold/geopolitical hedge, class: Commodity
```

ETF **look-through**: where the top holdings of an ETF are known and static
enough (e.g. SMH is semiconductors and includes SK Hynix), the theme exposure
adds the ETF's weight to that theme and flags single-name overlap. Look-through
data that is not reliably free is omitted and noted as a limitation, never
guessed.

## Rules (concentration flags)

Thresholds are explicit and configurable; defaults below. Every flag carries the
measured number and a limitation note.

| Flag | Condition (default) | Message intent |
|------|---------------------|----------------|
| 🔴 `SECTOR_OVERCONCENTRATED` | largest sector weight ≥ 70% | "X% in one sector; a sector shock hits almost the whole portfolio." |
| 🔴 `SINGLE_FACTOR` | single theme/factor weight ≥ 60% | "X% depends on one factor (e.g. memory cycle); MU, SKHY and SMH's memory sleeve move together." |
| 🟡 `TOP1_HEAVY` | top-1 holding ≥ 40% | "Top holding is X% of the portfolio." |
| 🟡 `HIGH_CORRELATION` | avg pairwise correlation ≥ 0.7 | "Holdings move together (avg corr X); diversification is lower than the count of positions suggests." |
| 🟡 `LOW_CASH_BUFFER` | cash weight < 10% | "Little dry powder for corrections." |
| ⚪ `WELL_DIVERSIFIED` | none of the above | informational |

Flags describe **current condition with evidence**; they never predict prices
and never say "sell". Suggested actions (e.g. "consider a low-correlation sector
ETF") come from the Opportunity Scanner module, cross-referenced.

## Resilience

- A ticker missing price history: volatility/correlation for it are reported as
  "insufficient history" and excluded from the correlation matrix, not guessed.
- If all history is unavailable, allocation/concentration still compute from
  weights; risk metrics degrade with a clear note.

## Test cases

Deterministic unit tests over fixed positions and fixed price series.

1. **TC-01 Weights sum to 100%.** Given 3 holdings + cash, computed weights
   (including cash) sum to 100% (±0.01).
2. **TC-02 Sector concentration.** Three holdings all sector IT at weights
   40/30/15 and 15% cash → `largest_sector_weight = 85%`, flag
   `SECTOR_OVERCONCENTRATED` raised.
3. **TC-03 Single-factor with look-through.** MU 10%, SKHY 10%, SMH 35% where
   SMH look-through assigns its weight to Semiconductors and 20% of it overlaps
   memory → memory-factor exposure computed and `SINGLE_FACTOR` evaluated
   against the 60% default.
4. **TC-04 HHI.** Two holdings 50/50 → `HHI = 5000`. One holding 100% →
   `HHI = 10000`. Four holdings 25% each → `HHI = 2500`.
5. **TC-05 Volatility.** A constant-price series → volatility `0`. A known
   synthetic return series → volatility equals the hand-computed value
   (±1e-6).
6. **TC-06 Max drawdown.** Series `[100, 120, 60, 90]` → max drawdown `-50%`
   (120 → 60).
7. **TC-07 Correlation.** Two identical series → correlation `1.0`; two exactly
   opposite series → `-1.0`; `HIGH_CORRELATION` raised when avg ≥ 0.7.
8. **TC-08 Relative return.** Portfolio −4.4% vs benchmark −4.1% over 1w →
   relative = −0.3%, reported as "in line with benchmark".
9. **TC-09 Low cash flag.** Cash weight 5% → `LOW_CASH_BUFFER` raised; 20% → not
   raised.
10. **TC-10 Insufficient history is excluded, not fatal.** A newly listed ticker
    (history < lookback) is excluded from volatility/correlation with a flagged
    note; other metrics still compute.
11. **TC-11 No price for a holding.** Price fetch fails for one ticker →
    allocation uses last known / flagged, risk metrics for it marked
    unavailable, run still produces a result.
12. **TC-12 Evidence attached.** Every raised flag includes the measured number
    and a limitation note (non-empty), enforcing the evidence-first contract.
