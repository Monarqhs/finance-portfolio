# 02. Macro & Currency

## Status

Draft — awaiting review. Second analysis module. Consumes FRED series
(`docs/01-data-sources/02. FRED (Macro).md`) and USD/IDR from the price source.
Deterministic and auditable; it explains the macro backdrop, it does not predict
it.

## Why this module

The owner observed that last week's broad sell-off was multi-factor: capital
rotating out of equities as **US and Japanese government bond yields rose**, plus
renewed fear of a 2008-style event framed as a **possible "AI bubble."** This
module turns that qualitative story into **measured, dated signals** tied to the
portfolio, so the report can say *why* a move likely happened — with evidence
and explicit limitations — instead of guessing.

## Inputs

- FRED series: `DGS10`, `DGS2`, `T10Y2Y`, `DFF`, `DTWEXBGS`, `VIXCLS`.
- USD/IDR current + ~1w/1m ago (price source).
- Portfolio theme exposure (from the Risk module) — to connect macro to *this*
  portfolio, not markets in the abstract.

## Outputs

A `MacroRead` result:

1. **Rates panel:** 10Y, 2Y, the 10Y−2Y spread, Fed Funds — each with
   week/month change and direction.
2. **Dollar & volatility panel:** broad dollar index and VIX, with changes.
3. **Currency panel:** USD/IDR level + week/month change, and a plain statement
   of its effect on USD-asset entry cost ("a weaker rupiah raises the IDR cost
   of buying USD assets").
4. **Regime tags** (rule-based, see Rules) — short labels like
   `YIELDS_RISING`, `CURVE_STEEPENING`, `RISK_OFF`, `DOLLAR_STRONG`.
5. **Portfolio linkage note:** connects the regime to the owner's exposure,
   e.g. "yields rising + ~100% semiconductor → the week's drawdown is consistent
   with a macro rotation out of growth, not a break in the holdings'
   fundamentals — evidence: 10Y +X bps, holdings fell together, trend still
   above SMA200. Limitation: macro attribution is contextual, not causal proof."

## Rules (regime tags)

Thresholds explicit and configurable; defaults below. Every tag carries the
measured number and a limitation note. These are **condition labels, not
forecasts**.

| Tag | Condition (default) | Reading |
|-----|---------------------|---------|
| `YIELDS_RISING` | 10Y up ≥ 15 bps over 1w | Headwind for growth/semis; a rotation driver. |
| `YIELDS_FALLING` | 10Y down ≥ 15 bps over 1w | Tailwind for growth/semis. |
| `CURVE_STEEPENING` / `CURVE_FLATTENING` | T10Y2Y change ≥ 10 bps | Growth/recession-expectation context. |
| `FED_TIGHTENING` / `FED_EASING` | DFF change over 1m | Policy-stance context. |
| `DOLLAR_STRONG` / `DOLLAR_WEAK` | DTWEXBGS change ≥ 1% over 1w | Backdrop for USD/IDR entry cost. |
| `RISK_OFF` | VIX ≥ 25 **or** VIX up ≥ 20% over 1w | Broad fear; contextualizes synchronized drawdowns. |
| `RUPIAH_WEAKENING` / `RUPIAH_STRENGTHENING` | USD/IDR change ≥ 1% over 1w | Direct effect on IDR entry cost / FX P&L. |

### "AI-bubble risk" context (measured, not predicted)

A **non-forecast** context block. It never claims a bubble will or will not pop;
it reports measurable proxies the reader can weigh:

- Concentration of the portfolio in one AI-adjacent theme (from the Risk
  module).
- Synchronized drawdown of holdings this week (they fell together → systemic,
  not idiosyncratic).
- `RISK_OFF` + `YIELDS_RISING` co-occurrence (the rotation pattern the owner
  described).
- **Explicit limitation line:** "These are current conditions and historical
  analogies, not a prediction. Analogies to 2008 are context, not a forecast."

## Resilience

- Any missing FRED series → that panel row is "unavailable" with a note; other
  rows still render.
- Missing USD/IDR → currency panel notes the gap; the ledger's per-transaction
  rates still support historical FX P&L.

## Test cases

Deterministic tests over fixture macro data.

1. **TC-01 Rates panel deltas.** Given fixture 10Y/2Y series, the panel shows
   correct latest values and 1w/1m changes.
2. **TC-02 `YIELDS_RISING` tag.** 10Y +18 bps over 1w → tag raised; +5 bps → not
   raised.
3. **TC-03 Curve tag.** T10Y2Y moves +12 bps → `CURVE_STEEPENING`; −12 bps →
   `CURVE_FLATTENING`.
4. **TC-04 `RISK_OFF` via level and via jump.** VIX = 27 → raised; VIX 18→22
   (+22%) → raised; VIX flat at 15 → not raised.
5. **TC-05 Rupiah tag.** USD/IDR 17000→17200 (+1.18%) → `RUPIAH_WEAKENING`.
6. **TC-06 Portfolio linkage requires risk input.** Given theme exposure ≥ 60%
   and `YIELDS_RISING`, the linkage note is produced with both numbers embedded.
7. **TC-07 AI-bubble block is non-forecast.** The generated block contains the
   explicit limitation line and contains no predictive phrasing (asserted
   against a banned-phrase list, e.g. "will crash", "going to").
8. **TC-08 Missing series is non-fatal.** VIX unavailable → `RISK_OFF` evaluated
   from available inputs or marked indeterminate with a note; other tags
   unaffected.
9. **TC-09 Evidence attached.** Every raised tag carries its measured number and
   a non-empty limitation note.
