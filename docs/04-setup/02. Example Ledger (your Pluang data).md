## Notes on the figures

- **Prices are in USD** (`price_usd`) taken from Pluang's "Harga Order", and
  **`usdidr` is the FX rate at execution** (Rp17,905) — this is what makes the
  asset-vs-FX P&L split accurate.
- **Fees** combine Pluang's "Biaya Transaksi" + "Pajak & Biaya Pihak Ketiga"
  (e.g. SMH: $0.57 + $0.17 = $0.74).
- **RDPU** is recorded as an **IDR-native row** (`currency = IDR`): `price_usd`
  holds the IDR value, `usdidr` is left blank (treated as 1), so it carries no
  fake FX exposure and contributes to the cash-buffer metric.
- When you add more transactions (DCA, selling, new tickers), just append rows.
  The pipeline recomputes weighted-average cost, realized/unrealized P&L, and
  the currency split automatically.
